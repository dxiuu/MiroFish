Simulate a political social media ecosystem where diverse actors — DC insider journalists, political analysts, prediction market traders, MAGA media defenders, government officials, and ordinary citizens — react to breaking news about Trump Cabinet member departures. The simulation should capture how information cascades from wire services through political Twitter to Reddit, how prediction market prices on Kalshi overshoot on breaking news due to momentum traders, and how mean-reversion traders and political analysts pull prices back toward fundamentals within hours. Key actors include named journalists (Maggie Haberman, Jonathan Swan, Kaitlan Collins), political analysts (Nate Silver, Larry Sabato), prediction market participants, MAGA media figures (Tucker Carlson, Sean Hannity, Steve Bannon), and congressional power brokers. The primary question: which Cabinet member departs first, and does social media sentiment accurately predict or distort the true departure probability?

Kalshi Market Structure: Contract "Who will leave Trump's Cabinet next?" on Kalshi (CFTC-regulated). $2.1M volume across 23 sub-markets. Current prices: Chavez-DeRemer 59c, Bondi 49c, Hegseth 45c, Gabbard 45c, RFK Jr ~22c, Rubio ~18c, Bessent ~14c, others 5-12c.

Trading Strategy to Evaluate (Overshoot-and-Fade): When breaking news causes a 10%+ price move in any Cabinet sub-market within 30 minutes, enter a counter-position assuming 60-70% mean reversion within 4-6 hours. Political news empirically causes initial overreaction of 8-15% beyond fair value.

Desired Agent Population Distribution: DC insider journalists 15% (fastest reactors, wire-service-driven); Political analysts 20% (slower, prior-anchored, reference historical base rates); Social media momentum traders 25% (fastest Kalshi traders, react to headlines not articles, overshoot causers); Mean-reversion arb traders 20% (fade overshoots, enter after momentum spike); Cross-market spillover traders 10% (trade correlation between sub-markets); Noise traders 10% (reactive to social media sentiment, amplify momentum).

Information Cascade Timing: Wire service breaks news -> political Twitter within 5 min -> prediction market Twitter reacts within 15 min -> momentum traders push Kalshi price within 30 min -> Reddit amplifies within 45 min -> analysts begin counter-narrative within 1-2 hours -> mean reversion begins within 3-6 hours -> price stabilizes at new equilibrium.
